Outbound voice campaigns

Voice campaigns that finish the conversation on WhatsApp.

EVRA is an outbound calling platform operated by GhostAI. Businesses use it to reach their customers by automated voice call, and to send the details straight to WhatsApp to anyone who asks for them on that call.

How it works

Three steps, and nothing sent without a request

Our clients upload their own customer lists and build a campaign in the EVRA dashboard. From there the flow is the same every time.

STEP 01

The call

The customer receives a call and hears a short recorded message about the client's product or service. The business is named up front.

STEP 02

The opt-in

The customer is asked to press 1 if they would like the details sent to them on WhatsApp. Pressing 1 is the only way a message is ever sent.

STEP 03

The WhatsApp

The information they asked for arrives on WhatsApp shortly after the call. They can reply, ask questions, or stop the messages at any point.

Consent and opt-out

We only message people who asked us to

No WhatsApp message is sent unless the customer pressed 1 on the call and asked for it. The recorded message states that the details will be sent on WhatsApp before the customer chooses, so the opt-in is specific and informed.

Anyone who asks to stop is removed immediately and permanently. An opt-out on a call is honoured on WhatsApp, and an opt-out on WhatsApp is honoured on the phone. We keep a suppression list so a number that has opted out cannot be added back by a later upload.

Who uses EVRA

Built for businesses with their own customer base

EVRA is used by South African businesses that need to reach customers who have already agreed to hear from them, and want a record of what happened on every contact.

  • Campaign setup, scheduling, and calling windows enforced per campaign
  • Live campaign monitoring and per-call outcomes
  • Reporting on calls placed, connected, opted in, and opted out
  • Automatic suppression of anyone who has asked not to be contacted
  • Each client sees only their own campaign data

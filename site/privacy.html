Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

This policy explains how GhostAI ("we", "us") handles personal information in the EVRA outbound calling platform. It covers both the businesses who use EVRA (our clients) and the members of the public those clients contact.

1. Our role

Our clients decide who to contact and why. They supply the contact lists and are the responsible party for that information. We process it on their behalf and under their instruction, as an operator. We do not sell contact information, buy it, or use one client's data for another client.

2. Information we process

  • Contact details supplied by our client, typically a name and mobile number
  • The outcome of each call, such as whether it was answered and how long it lasted
  • Keypad responses during the call, including a press of 1 to opt in and a press of 9 to opt out
  • Call recordings, where the campaign records calls and the recording is disclosed on the call
  • For WhatsApp messages: delivery and read status, replies you send us, and any request to stop
  • For dashboard users: name, email address, and sign-in activity

3. Why we process it

  • To place the calls our client has asked us to place
  • To send the WhatsApp message you requested when you pressed 1 on the call
  • To handle your replies to that message
  • To record and honour opt-out requests across both phone and WhatsApp
  • To report campaign results back to the client whose campaign it was
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations and to resolve complaints

4. WhatsApp messages

We send WhatsApp messages using the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. We only send a message to someone who pressed 1 on a call and asked for the details to be sent to them.

To deliver the message, your mobile number is shared with Meta. Meta returns delivery status, your replies, and any request you make to stop receiving messages. We use that information only to deliver the service described here and to report campaign results to the relevant client. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build advertising audiences from it, and we do not share it with anyone other than the client whose campaign you responded to.

Meta processes this information under its own terms. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

5. Opting out

You can stop being contacted at any time, in any of these ways:

  • Press 9 during a call
  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message we send you
  • Email garth@ghost-ai.co.za with the number to remove

An opt-out is applied across both channels: stopping the calls also stops the WhatsApp messages, and stopping the WhatsApp messages also stops the calls. Your number is added to a suppression list so that it cannot be re-added by a later upload from any client.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with the service providers needed to run the platform, and only for that purpose:

  • Meta Platforms, for delivering and receiving WhatsApp messages
  • Our telecommunications carriers, for placing calls
  • Our cloud hosting and database providers, for storing campaign data

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa. Where that happens we rely on the provider's contractual data protection commitments. We do not otherwise transfer personal information across borders.

7. How long we keep it

Campaign and call records are kept while the client's account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for reporting and dispute resolution. Call recordings are kept for a shorter period. Opt-out records are kept indefinitely, because we need them to keep honouring the opt-out. Clients may ask us to delete their campaign data, and we will do so except where we must retain something by law or to maintain a suppression record.

8. Security

Access to the dashboard requires an individual account, and each client can see only their own data. Data is encrypted in transit. Call recordings are stored in private storage and are only accessible through short-lived, signed links issued to authorised users.

9. Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is being used. Contact us at garth@ghost-ai.co.za and we will respond. Where the information was supplied by one of our clients, we may need to refer your request to that client as the responsible party, and we will tell you when we do.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the service changes, and the date at the top will change with it.
